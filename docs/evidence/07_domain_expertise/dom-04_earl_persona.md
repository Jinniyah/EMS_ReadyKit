# DOM-04: Earl Persona Document

Earl is a 68-year-old retired police chief who now serves as a supervisor on the Newberg Township EMS team, checking compliance dashboards and reviewing team readiness from his iPhone between other commitments. He's comfortable with authority and procedure, but not with apps — he didn't grow up on smartphones, has limited patience for menus or jargon, and will abandon a screen that makes him guess what to do next. Designing for Earl meant treating "obvious to Earl" as the bar for every screen: 60px tap targets so imprecise taps on a phone still land correctly, plain English labels instead of internal terms like "par level" or "reconciliation," and single-purpose screens that ask for one decision at a time instead of stacking multiple actions together. Earl's persona is the reason the app favors clarity over density everywhere — if a design choice would make sense to a developer but confuse Earl, the app defaults to Earl.
