1""" 

2models/item.py 

3Item — defines what is tracked in inventory. 

4 

5## Item check types — validated against Ambulance 712 inventory forms 

6 

7The real inventory forms for Ambulance 712 reveal four fundamentally 

8different kinds of checks a crew member performs. The check_type field 

9drives which fields are used during a daily check and which UI controls 

10the frontend renders. 

11 

12 SUPPLY (default) 

13 A counted or presence-verified supply item. 

14 Fields used: quantity_needed, quantity_found, lot_id (if expires) 

15 Examples: Kerlix (x3), Tourniquet (x2), OPAs/NPAs, Stethoscope 

16 

17 MEASUREMENT 

18 A numeric reading taken from equipment — not a count. 

19 Fields used: measurement_value (float), measurement_unit 

20 Examples: 

21 - O2 tank PSI (on-board O2, portable O2, stretcher O2, jump bag O2) 

22 - Glucometer test strip expiry (date component only) 

23 The "need" for a measurement is a minimum threshold (e.g. PSI >= 500) 

24 stored in ParLevel.min_quantity cast as a float. 

25 

26 FUNCTIONAL 

27 A pass/fail operational check — does the system work? 

28 Fields used: functional_pass (bool) 

29 Examples from Truck Operations section: 

30 - Runs and Starts 

31 - External Warning Systems (Lights & Sirens) 

32 - Patient compartment climate controlled 

33 - Communication - Medcom Compliant 

34 - Battery OK? (AED, LUCAS, stretcher) 

35 

36 DATE_RECORD 

37 A date is recorded — not a count, not a measurement. 

38 Fields used: date_value (date) 

39 Examples: 

40 - AED Date of Last Charge 

41 - LUCAS Device Date of Last Charge 

42 These are maintenance records. A supervisor sets a required 

43 recurrence interval; the check records the actual date. 

44 

45 DOCUMENT 

46 Presence-only check for required paperwork and reference materials. 

47 Fields used: quantity_found (0 = missing, 1 = present) — no lot tracking. 

48 Examples: PCR or HERN PCR, Billing Form, AMA, Protocol Book, 

49 Clipboard w/ Paperwork, Insurance Information 

50 

51## Category 

52 MEDICATION — controlled or non-controlled drugs (always SUPPLY check_type) 

53 CONSUMABLE — single-use supplies (SUPPLY check_type) 

54 EQUIPMENT — reusable gear; may be SUPPLY, MEASUREMENT, FUNCTIONAL, or DATE_RECORD 

55 DOCUMENT — paperwork; always DOCUMENT check_type 

56 

57## AED example — one piece of equipment, multiple items 

58The LifePak AED is modeled as four separate Item records in the same compartment: 

59 Item: "AED Battery" check_type=FUNCTIONAL → Battery OK? yes/no 

60 Item: "AED Date of Last Charge" check_type=DATE_RECORD → date recorded 

61 Item: "AED Pads Adult" check_type=SUPPLY → quantity + expiry date 

62 Item: "AED Pads Pediatric" check_type=SUPPLY → quantity + expiry date 

63 

64## O2 tank example — presence + PSI reading 

65 Item: "On-Board O2 Tank" check_type=SUPPLY → present yes/no (qty=1) 

66 Item: "On-Board O2 PSI" check_type=MEASUREMENT → PSI reading (e.g. 1800) 

67"""

94class ItemCheckType(str, enum.Enum): 

95 SUPPLY = "SUPPLY" # counted / presence-verified supply (default) 

96 MEASUREMENT = "MEASUREMENT" # numeric reading (PSI, temperature, glucose) 

97 FUNCTIONAL = "FUNCTIONAL" # pass/fail operational check (battery OK, runs & starts) 

98 DATE_RECORD = "DATE_RECORD" # date recorded (last charge date, last service date) 

99 DOCUMENT = "DOCUMENT" # presence-only paperwork check 

100 EXPIRY_DATE = "EXPIRY_DATE" # expiration date from package label (e.g. AED pads) — EXPIRED when today > date_value

109 item_id: Mapped[int] = mapped_column(primary_key=True, autoincrement=True)

129 # Controlled substances require ALS vehicle + dual-witness count

134 # Cabinet grouping for the Item Catalog UI (ITM-3). 

135 # One of seven values: "Airway & Respiratory", "Wound Care & Trauma Supplies", 

136 # "PPE & Cleaning", "Diagnostic & Monitoring Equipment", 

137 # "Medications & Controlled Substances", "Documents, Linens & Patient Comfort",

139 category_group: Mapped[Optional[str]] = mapped_column(String(100), nullable=True) 

140 

141 # Unit of measure for SUPPLY items (each, box, roll, mg, mL...) 

142 # For MEASUREMENT items this is the reading unit (PSI, °F, mg/dL...)

144 unit_of_measure: Mapped[str] = mapped_column(String(30), nullable=False) 

145 

146 # For MEASUREMENT items: the minimum acceptable value 

147 # e.g. O2 tank PSI minimum threshold of 500 

148 # Stored here so supervisors can configure per item, not per par level 

149 measurement_minimum: Mapped[Optional[float]] = mapped_column(nullable=True) 

150 

151 # For MEASUREMENT items: maximum acceptable value (optional) 

152 # e.g. blood glucose >400 mg/dL is critical high 

153 measurement_maximum: Mapped[Optional[float]] = mapped_column(nullable=True) 

154 

155 # For DATE_RECORD items: maximum number of days between required events 

156 # e.g. AED must be charged at least every 90 days → recurrence_days = 90 

157 recurrence_days: Mapped[Optional[int]] = mapped_column(nullable=True) 

158 

159 active: Mapped[bool] = mapped_column(Boolean, default=True, nullable=False) 

160 

161 # True = appears in station supply catalog (SR-B1). False for equipment items
