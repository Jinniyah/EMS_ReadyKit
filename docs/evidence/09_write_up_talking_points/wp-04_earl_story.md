# WP-04: The Earl Story

Early in design, I kept picturing Earl — a 68-year-old retired police chief who supervises the team from his iPhone and has no patience for figuring out an unfamiliar app — trying to approve a compliance check between other things he's doing that day. That image is what killed an early version of the compliance dashboard that packed vehicle status, item exceptions, and a "resolve" action onto one dense screen: it made sense to me as the developer, but it was exactly the kind of screen Earl would stare at and give up on. I split it into single-purpose screens — one to see status, one to drill into an exception, one to act on it — with plain-English labels and 60px tap targets throughout, because the real success metric for this app was never feature count, it was whether Earl could use it without calling me.
