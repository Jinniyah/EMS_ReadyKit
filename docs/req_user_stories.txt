Updated User Stories (Vehicle → Station Model)
🚑 Daily Operations

As a responder, I complete a daily inventory check for my assigned vehicle.
As a supervisor, I see which vehicles at my station are compliant today.

🧪 Expiration & Stock

As a supply officer, I see expiring items across all vehicles at a station.
As a supervisor, I get alerts when a vehicle drops below par.

🔐 Controlled Substances

As ALS crew, I complete a daily controlled substance check for my vehicle.
As supervisor, I must replace expiring controlled substances within 7 days and track discrepancies.


Mobile/Barcode

As a crew member, I can check items in/out using barcode scan to speed logging and reduce errors.
