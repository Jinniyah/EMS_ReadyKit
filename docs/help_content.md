## Tutorial (8 steps)
# Shown to new users on first login. Replayable from Help menu.
# Green tip boxes = helpful hints. Amber warning boxes = safety-critical.

### Step 1 — Welcome to EMS ReadyKit
This app replaces the paper inventory form. Every time you start a shift,
you'll use it to check that your vehicle is fully stocked and that nothing
is expired.

It takes about 10–15 minutes to do a full check. Your progress is saved
automatically, so if you get called away you won't lose anything.

TIP: You only need to do this on your phone or tablet — no computer needed.

---

### Step 2 — Pick your vehicle
When you open the app, you'll see a list of vehicles at your station. Tap
the one you're assigned to check today.

Under each vehicle, you'll see whether it's already been checked. If it
says "No check yet today" — that's your truck.

TIP: The date defaults to today. If you're doing a late entry from a paper
form, tap Edit to change it.

---

### Step 3 — Pick a compartment
After selecting your vehicle, you'll see a list of compartments — these
match the actual compartments on the truck, in order from front to back.

Tap the first one to open it. The number next to each compartment tells
you how many items are inside.

WARNING: Work through compartments in order — it matches how you physically
move around the truck.

---

### Step 4 — Count each item
Inside each compartment you'll see every item that should be there.
For each one:

1. Look at the Need number — that's how many should be there.
2. Count what's actually on the truck.
3. Tap + or − to enter your count.
4. Tap the checkmark to confirm you checked it.

The row turns green if you're good, amber if you're short, or red if
it's missing.

TIP: You can't skip items. Every row needs a checkmark before you can
move on.

---

### Step 5 — Expiry dates
For medications and other tracked items, you'll see a lot number and
expiry date. If the date has passed, the row shows EXPIRED in red.

If the date on the box is different from what the app shows, tap the [?]
next to the date to enter the correct date from the box.

If an item is expired, the app will ask if you replaced it. If yes, enter
the new lot info. If no, enter a brief reason.

WARNING: Never use an expired item in the field. Always flag it — even if
it's the only one you have.

---

### Step 6 — Controlled substances
Items marked with a red lock icon are controlled substances (narcotics and
certain medications). These need a second crew member to witness the count.

Make sure you add your second crew member on Step 1 before checking these
items. When you validate a controlled substance, a note is automatically
added to the record confirming who witnessed it.

TIP: Your supervisor has already set the required quantities. You're just
confirming what's there.

---

### Step 7 — Review and submit
When all compartments are done, tap "Review & submit". You'll see a summary
showing your name, the date, and any items that need attention.

If something looks wrong, tap Back to fix it. When everything looks right,
tap Submit check.

You'll get a confirmation screen with a check number — that's your record
that the check was done.

TIP: Once submitted, the check is locked. Only a supervisor can make changes
after that.

---

### Step 8 — That's it — you're ready!
The app will guide you through the same steps every time. After a few checks
it will feel very familiar.

If you ever get stuck, tap the ? button on any screen for plain-English help,
or open the FAQ from the Help menu.

You can replay this tutorial anytime from the Help menu.

TIP: The whole process usually takes 10–15 minutes. The more consistently
it's done, the more it protects you and your crew.

## FAQ — For crew members (responders)

Q: How do I start a daily check?
A: Open the app and tap Start check. Select your vehicle from the list —
make sure it says "No check yet today" underneath, which means it hasn't
been checked this shift. Then follow the steps: pick your compartment,
count each item, and submit when you're done.

---

Q: What do the colors mean?
A: Each item row is color-coded so you can see the status at a glance:
- GREEN (OK) — you have enough and nothing is expired.
- AMBER (Short) — you have some, but not the full required amount.
- RED (Missing) — the count is zero. This item is not on the truck.
- RED (Expired) — the lot number on this item has passed its expiration date.

---

Q: I left the app halfway through — did I lose my work?
A: No. The app saves your progress automatically after every item you check.
When you come back, you'll see a yellow "Draft saved" banner at the top of
the vehicle selection screen. Tap it to pick up right where you left off.

---

Q: What do I do if an item is expired?
A: First, validate the item as expired — tap the checkmark even though it's
red. The app will ask: "Was this item replaced?"

If you replaced it: enter the new lot number and expiry date from the
replacement package. The row will update.

If you couldn't replace it (no stock available): enter a brief reason.
Your supervisor will be notified automatically.

---

Q: What is the validate (checkmark) button for?
A: The checkmark button confirms you physically looked at that item. Every
item starts with a light red background to remind you it hasn't been checked
yet.

After you enter the count, tap the checkmark to record that you verified it.
The background will change to green, amber, or stay red depending on what
you found. You cannot finish a compartment until every item has been validated.

---

Q: The expiry date on the box doesn't match the app — what do I do?
A: Tap the [?] button next to the expiry date. A small panel will open
showing the system date and letting you enter the date on the box. Tap
"Use box date" — the app will re-check whether the item is expired based
on that date and record the discrepancy. Your supervisor will correct the
system record.

---

Q: Can I go back and fix something I already validated?
A: Yes, until you hit Submit check on the final screen. You can tap Back at
any time to return to a compartment and change a count or add a note. Once
the check is submitted, it is locked and only a supervisor can make corrections.

---

Q: An item on the truck isn't showing in the app — how do I add it?
A: Tap "Request item addition" at the bottom of any compartment screen. Fill
in the item name and reason (e.g. "New protocol — nitroglycerin added per
medical director order dated May 1"). Your supervisor will be notified and
will add the item to the system.

---

Q: The truck has a problem — how do I report it?
A: On the final review screen (Step 4), tap "Report a vehicle issue". Choose
the severity — Urgent means the vehicle may not be safe to deploy; Non-urgent
means it can wait for scheduled maintenance. Enter a brief description. Your
supervisor will be notified immediately for urgent issues.

---

Q: The check was done on paper — can I enter it for yesterday?
A: Yes. On Step 1 (vehicle selection), tap [Edit] next to the check date.
You can set the date up to 7 days in the past. Enter the data from the paper
form and submit as normal. The check will be recorded with that date.

## FAQ — For supervisors

Q: How do I see which trucks were checked today?
A: Your dashboard shows today's status as soon as you log in — "3 of 3
checks complete" or "1 of 3 — Unit 402 not checked yet." For a full history,
open the Compliance calendar from the dashboard. Each day shows a colored
cell per vehicle: green = passed, amber = issues found, red = failed or missed.

---

Q: How do I pull up a specific check for legal review?
A: Open the Compliance calendar, navigate to the date in question, and tap
the vehicle's cell. You'll see the full check record including: who performed
it, the second crew member, every item counted, lot numbers, expiry dates,
and any notes.

Tap Print to open the browser print dialog. The printed version includes a
document ID, who generated it, and a timestamp — suitable for legal hold.

---

Q: A check came back as FAIL — what do I do?
A: Open the check from your dashboard alert or the calendar. Review the
flagged items. Once you've addressed the issue, tap "Acknowledge and record
action" and describe what you did (e.g. "Replaced expired Epi 1:10,000,
lot XYZ, expiry 2027-08-01, at 09:15am").

This acknowledgement is recorded in the audit trail and links your name
and timestamp to the corrective action.

---

Q: How do I mark a vehicle as out of service?
A: Go to Vehicles in the supervisor menu and tap the vehicle. Toggle it to
Inactive and enter a reason (e.g. "Brake repair — ETA 3 days"). While
inactive, the vehicle is grayed out for crew members and the calendar shows
it as a gray stripe — no check is required.

---

Q: How do I add a new crew member to the system?
A: From your dashboard, tap Request new user. Fill in the person's name,
email address, role (Responder or Supervisor), and their start date. The
system administrator will be notified and will set up their account. The
new crew member will receive an invitation email.

## Contextual help — per screen
# Shown when user taps the ? button on each screen.

### Screen: Select vehicle (Step 1)
This screen shows all active vehicles at your station. Tap the one you are
about to check. You can only select one vehicle at a time.

Look for the status under each vehicle name. "No check yet today" means it's
waiting to be done. "Checked at 06:15am" means someone already completed it
this shift — you don't need to do it again unless your station requires two
checks per day.

The check date at the bottom of the screen defaults to today. If you're
entering a check from a paper form done earlier, tap Edit to change it.

---

### Screen: Select compartment (Step 2)
This screen lists every compartment on the vehicle in the order they appear
physically — start from the top and work your way down. The number of items
in each compartment is shown so you know how long it will take.

Tap a compartment to open it and start counting. When you're done with a
compartment, tap Save compartment and it will show as green (no issues) or
red (issues found) in this list.

You can do compartments in any order and come back to any one at any time
before you submit.

---

### Screen: Count items (Step 3)
Each row is one item. The Need number shows how many should be on the truck.
Use the − and + buttons to enter how many you actually found. Then tap the
checkmark button to confirm you checked it.

The row color tells you the result: green means you're good, amber means
you're short, red means it's missing or expired. If an item is expired,
you'll be asked whether you replaced it.

Items with a red lock icon are controlled substances — a second crew member
must witness the count. Make sure you've added them on Step 1 before
validating these items.

You cannot move to the next screen until every item has a checkmark.

---

### Screen: Review and submit (Step 4)
This screen shows a summary of everything you recorded. Your name appears
under "Submitted by" — this is pulled from your login and cannot be changed.

If there are any issues (expired, missing, or short items), they are listed
here so you can review them before sending. You can tap Back to fix anything.

Once you tap Submit check, the record is locked. Your supervisor will be
automatically notified if any items are flagged as failed.

## Implementation notes for developers

- Tutorial: shown automatically on first login (localStorage flag: ems_tutorial_complete)
  Replayable from: Help menu → Replay tutorial
  Skip button: available on every step (sets the flag without completing)

- FAQ: searchable via client-side filter on question text
  Organized into two role sections: crew / supervisor
  FAQ section headers hide when search returns no results in that section

- Contextual help: triggered by ? button on each wizard step
  Opens as a bottom sheet (slides up from bottom, dismisses on tap outside)
  Content keyed by screen name constant (SCREEN_VEHICLE, SCREEN_COMPARTMENT, etc.)

- All help content lives in: src/modules/help/content.js (single source of truth)
  Imported by tutorial, FAQ, and contextual help components
  Never hardcode help text inline in components

- Reading level target: Grade 6–8 (Flesch-Kincaid)
  No acronyms without explanation on first use
  No medical jargon in UI-facing text (use "lot number" not "NDC", "expiry date" not "beyond-use date")
